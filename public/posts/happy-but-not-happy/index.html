Happy but not happy

May 28, 2026

A Lot of People Are Pretending To Be Happy Online#

And honestly, maybe we should be a little kinder to each other because of that.

The internet makes happiness look constant.

Perfect photos. Perfect relationships. Perfect productivity. Perfect confidence.

But real life does not work like that.

Some people smile online while silently dealing with:

  • anxiety
  • loneliness
  • burnout
  • family pressure
  • financial stress
  • fear about the future
  • AI AI AI AI

And you would never know.

Because social media trained everyone to hide the messy parts.

People post highlights during moments they barely survived.

Someone uploads gym progress while struggling mentally. Someone posts career wins while feeling completely lost. Someone tweets jokes every day because humor is easier than honesty.

Not everything online is fake. But not everything online is complete either.

And I think we forget that sometimes.

We compare our full reality to someone else’s edited moments and then wonder why we feel behind.

The truth is: most people are trying their best with the emotional weight they carry privately.

So this is just a reminder:

Be gentler with people. And be gentler with yourself too.

You do not know what someone is fighting quietly.

The happiest-looking person in the room might simply be the best at hiding it.

You’re Strong!!!
